INTERPRETATION
Prior training did not eliminate the network's ability to learn Task B.
However, the continued learner showed slower early adaptation, a slightly
higher final Task B error, and substantially less reliable attainment of
the prespecified performance threshold than the fresh learner. Adaptation
to Task B was also accompanied by substantial loss of Task A performance.
